# BNB Chain nodes: publicnode for recent blocks, Alchemy for history

- **Date:** 21 November 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decided by:** William
- **Builds on:** `2026-11-20-circuit-index.md` (the tape-out scan and the follow), `2026-11-20-market-index.md`
- **Changes:** where `BNB_ARCHIVE_RPC_URL` points; adds `BNB_RECENT_RPC_URL`

## Context

On 21 Nov 2026 the NodeReal archive node behind `BNB_ARCHIVE_RPC_URL` refused every call: "You've reached your monthly quota limit". The follow, the results job and the result page all stopped.

William's Alchemy key on BNB Smart Chain mainnet was probed the same day:

- **Reads at old blocks work.** `totalVerifWeight` at block 125,000,000 answered.
- **Logs are limited on the free tier:** `eth_getLogs` covers at most 10 blocks per request. The scans read up to 50,000 blocks at a time.

Alchemy's free tier also allows about 500 compute units a second and 30 million a month, at about 26 units per `eth_call`. A live follow through it was refused with HTTP 429 after 200 of 1,496 processors. Its quota can't carry Foreman's jobs either:

- the 5-minute follow reads every processor, about 11 million units a day;
- the hourly results rebuild makes about 80,000 calls, about 50 million units a day.

`bsc-rpc.publicnode.com` is free, has no key, and takes batches of 50. Measured on 21 Nov 2026:

- **State:** it served `eth_call` at 100, 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 blocks behind the head. From 20,000 back it refused with an error ("Archive requests require a personal token"), never a wrong answer.
- **Logs** in 200-block windows of BEM transfers: 131, 310 and 246 at 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 blocks behind the head. Further back the counts were 5 at 20,000, 0 at 30,000, then 22, 223 and 14 at 50,000, 80,000 and 120,000. On 19 Nov it had returned an empty list, not an error, for logs older than a few hours (CLAUDE.md, "RPC correction"). So its backends keep different amounts of log history, and an empty answer for an older range can't be trusted.

Almost every read the jobs and the page make is at a recent block: the follow's finalized head, the index block, and the market block, all minutes old.

## Options

| | Option | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | **Alchemy + publicnode** | Free; no change to the scans | Two nodes; old reads are slow on Alchemy's limits |
| B | Top up NodeReal | No code change | Costs money each month; the same wall next month |
| C | Alchemy's paid tier | One node, wide log reads | Costs money |

## Decision

**A**, chosen by William. He also chose to send recent reads, not only recent logs, to publicnode, once Alchemy's quota was measured. One BNB client, built in one place (`bnbClient` in `packages/chain-bnb`), routes each call by its block:

1. **Within 5,000 blocks of the head** (about 40 minutes), or at a tag such as `latest` or `finalized`: `BNB_RECENT_RPC_URL`, publicnode by default. Logs go in 200-block chunks. That is half of what publicnode was measured to serve. The 5-minute follow reads about 650 blocks.
2. **Older:** `BNB_ARCHIVE_RPC_URL`, Alchemy. Logs go in 10-block chunks, which is exact history. This happens only when a job has fallen behind by more than 40 minutes, or for a backfill. A log range that crosses the line is split.
3. **No block** (a receipt or transaction by hash): Alchemy, which has all history.
4. Log chunks are joined in block order. A failed chunk fails the whole read, never a partial list.

The head is publicnode's, read again when it is more than 30 seconds old, one read at a time shared by every call waiting on it (a read per call set off a stampede that publicnode throttled). Alchemy gets small batches (5) and patient retries, because its free tier refuses bursts.

## Free alternatives checked, 21 Nov 2026

William asked for a single free node like NodeReal. Two were tested with `pnpm --filter @foreman/index probe-node` (`packages/index/scripts/probe-bnb-node.ts`, which compares a node's TapedOut and Sold counts with the index). Neither fits, so A stays:

- **dRPC, free key:** state about 10,000 blocks back, older refused ("Unknown state"); log reads at most 100 blocks; batches at most 3 requests.
- **Blockmachine, no key:** logs for the last 100 blocks only; every state read failed ("missing trie node"). Its free key has full history, but its pricing page allows 10,000 requests a month, about 1% of Alchemy's free tier.

## NodeReal back as the archive node, 22 Nov 2026

A follow through Alchemy fell about 122,000 blocks behind and would have taken 4 to 6 hours to catch up. William took a NodeReal key again and set it as `BNB_ARCHIVE_RPC_URL`. The routing is unchanged; only the archive node is different:

- **`BNB_ARCHIVE_LOG_BLOCKS`** sets the blocks per log read on the archive node. It is 10 when not set (Alchemy's free tier), and 50,000 for NodeReal.
- Checked on 22 Nov 2026: BEM's supply at block 125,000,000 matched Alchemy's answer, and a 50,000-block log read of the mining contract returned 12,532 logs in 1.9 s.
- The follow reached the market stage in 117 s, where the Alchemy run had taken 37 minutes for a third of the blocks.

## What follows

- **`.env`:** `BNB_ARCHIVE_RPC_URL` is now William's Alchemy BNB mainnet URL. `BNB_RECENT_RPC_URL` is optional and defaults to `https://bsc-rpc.publicnode.com`.
- **The scans stay as they are.** Their spans are cut by the client, not by each scan.
- **Revisit** if the follow keeps falling behind, which makes Alchemy's slow path the common one. Revisit also if publicnode starts refusing the jobs' load, or if Alchemy's free compute units run out.
